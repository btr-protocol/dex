So the shape zoo above is gas-flat: every profile swaps at the same ~170–190k.

Verdict — we do NOT win on raw swap gas today, and shouldn't claim to. The general-AMM floor is ~95–110k, of which ~40–65k is unavoidable (21k base tx + 2× ERC-20 transfer). BTR sits ~2× that because every swap also reads an external oracle, computes a volatility-scaled fee, and applies coverage/LVR protection — active features a passive CPMM doesn't have, not waste. The quartic closes part of the gap (math now cheaper than Curve's Newton and Uniswap's tick traversal) but not all of it. Two structural facts: (1) singleton + transient-storage flash-accounting is now table-stakes (v4 / Balancer v3 / Fluid / Ekubo all do it); our flat-Pool + EIP-1167-clone arch does not, a standing ~20–40k disadvantage. (2) For stable-stable specifically, Fluid DEX Lite's ~10k is a real threat — it wins by dropping general curve math near peg. Where BTR actually competes is LP net-APR after LVR (oracle-priced, arb-resistant) and arbitrary liquidity shaping (this zoo) — not gas. To contend on gas would need a near-peg fast-path (skip oracle/coverage when |offset|<ε) + a singleton refactor — both post-launch.
